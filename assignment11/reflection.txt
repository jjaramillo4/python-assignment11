Task 4: Reflections

Static vs. interactive visualizations
Depending on the audience and the information you are trying to convey, either static or
interactive visualizations can be appropriate. For example, for reporting in an academic
paper or a snapshot to leadership, you can share a static report. If you are exploring data,
or showing complex data such as KPIs, you can use an interactive dashboard.

Advantages of dashboards for real-time data exploration
As mentioned before, dashboards can be interactive and allow users to explore complex data
and filter it to display specific information. They are very useful for data analysis and
for gathering insights from the data itself.

How interactive tools like Plotly improve data communication professionally
They allow users to see the data visually, and depending on the chart, they can convey the
significance of the data, show relationships within it, and support data analysis.
